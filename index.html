Read the $1Q vision

2030B is not a company, not a movement, not a manifesto. It is a registry — a public catalogue of the twenty-five departments a coherent civilization actually needs once it accepts that it is responsible for itself. Some departments protect existence (Quantum Ethics, Neural Sovereignty, Existential Risk, Planetary Defense, Civilizational Continuity). Some ground it (Ontology, Reality Verification, AI Alignment). Some carry it forward (Temporal Architecture, Post-Biological Integration, Cosmic Heritage).

Each department launches with a clear mandate, a five-detail blueprint, and a daily-cost figure attached. Together, the entire civilizational stack runs on less than $17.49 per human per day. “Nothing is impossible — only incapable people. With 2030B, there are only capables.”
