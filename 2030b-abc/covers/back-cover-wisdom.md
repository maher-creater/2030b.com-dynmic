# Back-Cover Wisdom — Maher's Words That Print on the Reader's Mind
**Book:** كن أعقل · Be More Sane · Book 1 of the Rushd Trilogy
**Author:** Maher © 2026

> *These are the words typeset inside the gold-ruled frame on the back cover.
> They are printed in 22pt naskh-modern Arabic at the top, followed by a thin
> gold rule, then the English at 14pt Inter Light. The reader closes the book
> on these lines; they remain in the mind long after the paper is shelved.*

---

## 1) The opening line — large, centered, Arabic only

> **«كُن أَعْقَل.**
> **فإنّ العَقْلَ جوازُك إلى الإنسانية،**
> **والحَقَّ ختمُه الذي لا يُزوَّر.»**

---

## 2) The five vows — set as a list, gold bullets

> **خمسةُ عهودٍ تَخرج بها من هذا الكتاب:**
>
> ① **لن أُصدِّق ما لم أَزِنه** — حتى لو نَطق به الجميع.
> ② **لن أَكذِب على نفسي** — فأوّلُ كذبةٍ تَهدم البيت من داخله.
> ③ **لن أَتبع قطيعًا** — ولو سار نحو نهرٍ من ذهب.
> ④ **لن أَخاف إلّا اللهَ** — فمَن خافه استراح من ألف خوفٍ صغير.
> ⑤ **لن أَموت قبل أن أُورِث عقلًا** — لا مالًا، ولا اسمًا، ولا حُلمًا فقط.

---

## 3) The middle aphorism — the line readers will quote

> **«ليس بينك وبين الرُّشدِ سنوات،**
> **بل قرارٌ واحدٌ في صفحةٍ واحدةٍ من كتابٍ واحد.**
> **وقد فُتِحتْ.»**

---

## 4) The economic truth — small, italic, justified

> «ثلاثون دولارًا ثَمنُ هذا الكتاب — ثَمنُ جوازِ سفرٍ في أكثر دول الأرض.
> اشتريتَه بدولاراتٍ، وستَدفع ثمنه الحقيقي بساعاتِ صدق.
> عشرةٌ بالمئة تَذهب إلى المؤلف، وتسعون تَذهب إلى الكوكب.
> فإنّ كلَّ نسخةٍ في يدِك هي عَمودٌ في إحدى الإدارات الخمسِ والعشرين
> التي ستَحرس حضارةَ ما بعدَ 2030.»

---

## 5) The closing line — bottom, smallest, but sharpest

> **«لم تَقرأ هذا الكتاب لتَعرِف.**
> **قَرأتَه لتُصبِح.**
> **والآن، فلتَبدأ.»**
> — **ماهر**

---

## 6) English mirror panel (smaller, beneath the gold rule)

> *"Be more sane.
> Reason is your passport to humanity;
> truth is the seal upon it that no forger can copy."*
>
> **Five vows you take by closing this book:**
> ① I will not believe what I have not weighed — even if everyone speaks it.
> ② I will not lie to myself — the first lie is the wall that falls inward.
> ③ I will not follow a herd — even if it walks toward a river of gold.
> ④ I will fear nothing but God — whoever fears Him rests from a thousand small fears.
> ⑤ I will not die before I bequeath a mind — not wealth, not a name, not a dream alone.
>
> *"Between you and rushd lies not years — but a single decision, on a single page, of a single book.
> That page has now been opened."*
>
> *"You did not read this book to know.
> You read it to become.
> Now — begin."*
>
> — **Maher**

## 7) Tiny dedication footline (publisher metadata strip)

> *For every adult on Earth who suspected they were sleeping. — M.*

## Typesetting notes (for the printer / typographer)

- **Arabic font**: Amiri Quran (display) for headlines · Noto Naskh Arabic for body.
- **English font**: Cormorant Garamond (display) · Inter Light (body).
- **Gold ink**: Pantone 871 C (metallic gold) on the obsidian #04030a stock.
- **Rose accent**: only on the gold rule corners and the ①–⑤ glyphs.
- **Quote glyphs**: « and » (Arabic guillemets) in 130% gold; " and " (English curly) in 110% cream.
- **Line spacing**: 1.4× for Arabic body, 1.55× for English body.
- **Margins**: 18mm outer, 22mm inner (binding side).
- **Spine alignment**: the closing line "والآن، فلتَبدأ" should sit exactly 38mm above the bottom edge, so it aligns horizontally with the front cover's author signature.

---

> *These words must print on the reader's mind as legibly as they print on the paper.
> That is the only test of a back cover worth setting in gold.*
