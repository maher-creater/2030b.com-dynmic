# Front Cover — Image Prompt
**Book:** كن أعقل · Be More Sane · Book 1 of the Rushd Trilogy
**Author:** Maher © 2026

## Primary prompt (for nano-banana-pro / gpt-image-2, aspect 2:3)
A monumental front-cover book design, portrait orientation, museum-grade typography.

Background: deep obsidian black (#04030a) with a faint conic-gradient halo radiating from upper-third center — gold (#d4a857), rose (#f5a3c0), indigo (#7c8df5), sage (#86c5a0) — opacity 18%, evoking a planetary sunrise seen from orbit.

Center composition: a single luminous **brass-gold scale (الميزان)**, perfectly balanced, suspended in negative space. The two pans of the scale each hold one object: on the left pan, a small **closed iron padlock** (representing the un-awakened mind); on the right pan, an **open key of pure light** (representing rushd — the awakened mind). The key glows brighter, slightly heavier, tilting the balance toward awakening. Subtle Arabic geometric pattern (eight-pointed star repeating) etched as a watermark across the lower third at 6% opacity.

Typography (Arabic dominant, English subordinate):
- **كن أعقل** — top third, centered, in elegant naskh-modern serif (similar to Cormorant Garamond Arabic or Amiri Quran), 180 pt, in burnished gold #d4a857 with a thin 1px rose outline, letter-spacing slightly open. Each letter feels carved from a single ingot.
- Underneath in smaller English, 36 pt thin sans-serif (Inter Light): "Be More Sane"
- Bottom third: **ماهر** in handwritten ink-brush calligraphy, 64 pt, off-white #f5e6b3
- Beneath that, in tiny English Inter 14 pt tracking-wide: "BOOK ONE · THE RUSHD TRILOGY · 2030B ECOSYSTEM"
- Top-left chip: a small circular emblem with "2030B" inscribed, gold rim, dark interior

Lighting: single soft spotlight from upper-left at 25° creating a gentle vignette toward the corners. The scale catches the light on its leftmost edge; the key throws a faint gold reflection onto the title.

Texture: subtle linen weave at 4% opacity over the entire surface, suggesting hardcover binding. Tiny gold-flake speckles (less than 0.3px each) scattered in the orb area — like dust of light. No bevels, no skeuomorphic gloss, no Photoshop filters. Museum-grade restraint.

Color palette (strict):
- Background: #04030a
- Gold primary: #d4a857
- Rose accent: #f5a3c0
- Indigo accent: #7c8df5
- Sage accent: #86c5a0
- Cream type: #f5e6b3

Avoid: skulls, swords, doves, brains, lightbulbs, mountains, eyes-in-triangles, any cliché. No human faces. No flags. No religious symbols other than the abstract eight-pointed star pattern.

Render style: matte print, high contrast, suitable for both physical hardcover (300 dpi CMYK) and retina digital (2x). Final image should feel like a Penguin Press × Tang Dynasty illuminated manuscript collaboration — quiet, expensive, eternal.

no text errors, no garbled Arabic, no extra fingers, no watermarks, no stock-photo people, no neon, no glitch, no anime, no comic style, no shutterstock vibe, no 3d render plastic look
