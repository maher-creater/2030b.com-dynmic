# Back Cover — Image Prompt
**Book:** كن أعقل · Be More Sane · Book 1 of the Rushd Trilogy
**Author:** Maher © 2026

## Primary prompt (for nano-banana-pro / gpt-image-2, aspect 2:3)
A monumental back-cover book design, portrait orientation, same museum-grade typography system as the front, mirrored and quieted.

Background: same deep obsidian black (#04030a) as the front cover, but the upper conic-halo has migrated to the *lower-right* corner, dimmer (12% opacity), as if the planetary sunrise on the front has now set on the back. A thin vertical line of gold light, 1px wide, runs from the upper-left to lower-right corner — a meridian, suggesting a passport's security thread.

Center-upper composition: a single **open hand**, palm facing the viewer, rendered as a fine line drawing in burnished gold #d4a857 against the black field. The hand is empty but the fingers are slightly cupped, as if having just released something. Above the palm, suspended in mid-air, a constellation of **25 small luminous dots** arranged in five tiers (7-6-5-4-3, matching the five priority levels of 2030B), each dot a different jewel-tone (gold, rose, indigo, sage, cyan). Faint lines connect them into an asterism — the silent map of the twenty-five departments. No labels. The reader recognises it only if they have read the book.

Middle band — a quote panel:
A thin gold-rule rectangle (1px stroke, #d4a857, 70% opacity) frames an empty inner area where the publisher will typeset Maher's back-cover wisdom (see `back-cover-wisdom.md`). The frame's four corners carry tiny eight-pointed star ornaments. The inner area is intentionally left blank in the cover art so the typographer can drop the text in at print time. (For the AI rendering, fill it with placeholder Arabic lorem text in faint gray at 25% opacity, so the layout is visible.)

Lower band:
- Left: a small ISBN barcode placeholder block, rendered as a stylised gold rectangle 40×20mm with horizontal gold lines, on a slightly lighter charcoal panel
- Right: the **2030B emblem** — a 2×2 matrix of four small squares (gold / rose / indigo / sage) inside a thin gold circle, 28mm diameter
- Center bottom: in Inter 11pt tracking-wide cream #f5e6b3, the single line: **2030B ECOSYSTEM · EDUCATION DEPARTMENT · MAHER © 2026 · ALL RIGHTS RESERVED**
- Beneath that, even smaller (8pt): "Purchase via Binance Pay · $30 USD · 10% royalty to author · 90% funds the twenty-five departments"

Spine indication (left edge, 12mm strip):
Vertical typography running bottom-to-top, gold on black: "كن أعقل · BE MORE SANE · MAHER · 2030B"

Lighting and texture identical to the front: matte linen weave 4%, gold-flake dust in the halo area, single 25° soft spotlight now from the upper-right (mirroring the front).

Color palette (strict, identical to front):
- Background: #04030a
- Gold primary: #d4a857
- Rose accent: #f5a3c0
- Indigo accent: #7c8df5
- Sage accent: #86c5a0
- Cream type: #f5e6b3

Avoid: photographs, human faces, busy patterns, multiple competing focal points, drop shadows, gradient buttons, web-UI elements. The back must feel quieter than the front, the way the closing of a door is quieter than its opening.

Render style: matte print, high contrast, suitable for 300 dpi CMYK physical hardcover and retina digital. Final image should feel like the back of a Penguin Press × Folio Society limited edition — restrained, expensive, finished.

## Negative prompt
no text errors, no garbled Arabic, no extra fingers on the hand, no eyes, no human faces other than the disembodied hand, no skulls, no doves, no flags, no shutterstock, no neon, no glitch, no 3d plastic gloss, no chromatic aberration

## Aspect / size
- Aspect ratio: **2:3** (standard hardcover)
- Resolution: 4K (3000 × 4500)
- Model: `nano-banana-pro` for typography fidelity; fallback `gpt-image-2`
