2030B is not a company, not a movement, not a manifesto. It is a registry — a public catalogue of the departments a coherent civilization actually needs once it accepts that it is responsible for itself.

Some departments protect existence (Quantum Ethics, Neural Sovereignty, Existential Risk). Some ground it (Ontology, Reality Verification). Some carry it forward (Temporal Architecture, Post-Biological Integration). Some preserve it (Collective Memory, Cosmic Heritage). Some keep it consistent (Paradox Resolution, Interspecies Communication).

Each department is launched as a WebBook, with a clear mandate and a five-detail blueprint. This site is the entry point — the first door a reader crosses to see the whole stack at once.
