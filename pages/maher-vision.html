From trillions to a quadrillion.

Today's world economy is roughly ~$110 trillion. To call it "an economy of trillions" is correct; to call it "a quadrillion economy" is fantasy — quadrillion does not exist yet. 2030B's claim is not that the number can be conjured by inflation. The claim is that twenty-five coordinated departments, each with a clear mandate and a published cost, can compound real value until the unit is reached.

Three multipliers

  1. 1 Energy & Data Commons remove rent from the two most expensive scarcities in modern economies. Marginal cost of intelligence and electricity falls toward zero; productive surface expands by an order of magnitude.
  2. 2 AI Alignment, Synthetic Empathy & Reality Verification make synthetic minds trustworthy enough to participate as economic agents. Trust is the largest untapped multiplier in human history.
  3. 3 Civilizational Continuity, Existential Risk & Planetary Defense re-price tail risk. When civilization stops paying its catastrophe-insurance premium in lost generations, the discount rate collapses and long-horizon investment becomes rational again.

Why this is not inflation

The $1Q figure is a productive number, not a monetary one. It reflects new categories of value (heritable-edit reversibility, neural-sovereignty insurance, sacred-arts heritage, deliberative civic trust) that today's GDP barely measures. 2030B does not print the money. It builds the measurable surface.

What "capable" means

Maher's wager is simple: the bottleneck has never been resources. It has been capability + coordination. 2030B is the coordination layer. The departments are the capability layer. Together: there are only capables.
