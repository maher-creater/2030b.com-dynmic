WebBook Standard v1

A WebBook is the canonical charter of a department.

Every department in 2030B publishes a WebBook: a living, navigable, AI-aware document that defines who they are, what they are responsible for, and how they intend to discharge that responsibility. WebBooks replace the brittle PDF, the dead whitepaper, the static "about" page.

The eight required sections

§1

Identity

Name, slug, level, color, anchor symbol, and one-line definition.

§2

Mandate (5 keys)

The five-detail blueprint exactly as stated in the official Registry.

§3

Foundations

The conceptual ground: what the department assumes about the world.

§4

Methods

How the department actually does its work — protocols, instruments, audits.

§5

Boundaries

What the department refuses to do, and where it defers to other departments.

§6

Roadmap

Public milestones, dependencies, and the next twelve months of work.

§7

Glossary

Terms a reader must know to use the WebBook responsibly.

§8

Anchor

The non-relativizable reference (for the Ontology Department, this is Al-Ḥaqq).
